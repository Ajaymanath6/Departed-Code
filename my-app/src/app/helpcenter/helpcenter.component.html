Case Search

Case Search allows you to find cases from a comprehensive database covering over 4,000 courts. Use it to quickly locate relevant cases, review details, and access the information you need across jurisdictions. Case search has three different modes to help you find relevant cases.

Natural Language Search

UniCourt DART’s Natural Language Search allows you to query our entire library of cases using everyday, conversational language instead of formal keywords or complex syntax. UniCourt then converts your natural language into our advanced query language to best find the cases you’re looking for. Below are some examples of natural language searches:

  • “Show me cases where John Smith is a defendant in California”
  • “Cases with John Doe or Jane Doe in New York filed in 2025”
  • E“Find cases where a summary judgement was granted on a rear end auto case in los angeles county, CA ”

Filter Search

UniCourt DART’s Filter Search allows you to narrow down results by applying specific criteria and parameters. This is a simplified version of our advanced query language that is suitable for most users. Simply select the filters you want to use and fill in the information you wish to search by.

Advanced Search

UniCourt DART’s Advanced Search combines keywords with operators to refine and control search results. This is our most advanced and complex search. To perform an advanced search you can enter the advanced query language directly into the search bar or open our Query Builder to construct an advanced search.

Case Search Results

After running a Case Search, your results are displayed in two flexible views to help you analyze and navigate cases efficiently.

Data View presents results in a table format, listing cases by name with customizable metadata fields such as case number, filing date, case status, court type, court name, court system, case class, area of law, and case type. This view is ideal for sorting, comparing, and scanning large sets of results.

Case Card View provides a more visual summary of each case. Every card includes the case name, case number, court, filing date, case type, and case status. It also highlights relevant details based on your search criteria. For example, if you search for a specific case event like a summary judgment, the card will display the related docket text or document. If you search by judge, the card will show the associated judge from the case metadata to help you quickly assess relevance. This is the recommended DART experience.

All results are automatically sorted by the most recent filing date and can be refined using filters such as state, court system, filing date, and case type. You can also save any search after execution using the Save Search button, preserving the exact parameters and format for easy access later.

Recent/Saved Searches

The Recent Searches feature automatically displays your 20 most recent searches, making it easy to revisit and rerun queries without starting from scratch.

Saved Searches allow you to store searches for future use. You can save a search directly from the search results page or by clicking the three dots next to a recent search and selecting save. Each saved search preserves the exact parameters and search format, so you can quickly access and reuse them whenever needed.

Sharable Case Search URLs

You can easily share your search results with other users by copying the URL from the case search results page and sending it to another user. They will be able to instantly run the same search on their account by pasting the URL into their browser search bar.

Case Page

To access a Case Page, click on any case from your Case Search results. The case will open in a new tab, allowing you to continue working without losing your search.

A Case Page is a standardized view of a litigation matter that brings together all available information about a lawsuit in one place. At the top of the page, you’ll see the case name as provided by the court, along with notifications showing when the case was last refreshed and when a new update was last found. Cases are automatically refreshed each time you open them. If new activity is detected, you’ll be prompted to refresh the page to load the latest updates, which are then added seamlessly to the case. From the top of the page, you can also set a Case Track to receive notifications about updates and pin the case to bookmark it for quick access later.

Below, you’ll find a series of tabs that organize the case details, including Summary, Docket Entries, Documents, Parties & Attorneys, Judges, Related Cases, Docket History, and Similar Cases. Each tab provides a deeper view into specific aspects of the case, making it easy to navigate and analyze the information you need.

Summary Tab

The Summary tab provides a high-level overview of the case, bringing together key information in one place. It includes important case metadata such as case number, court, and case type, along with a list of the top parties and attorneys involved. You’ll also find a recent case history section that highlights the latest filings, helping you quickly understand the most recent activity in the case.

Docket Entries Tab

The Docket Entries tab provides a complete list of all filings in a case, with the most recent entries displayed at the top. Up to 100 docket entries are shown per page, and additional entries are accessible through pagination. This list is fully text-searchable, allowing you to quickly locate specific case events such as a trial or complaint. When available, links to associated documents appear on the right side of each entry, making it easy to view or order documents directly from the docket.

Documents Tab

The Documents tab provides a complete list of all documents filed in the case. By default, documents are sorted with the most recent first, but you can reorder the list by clicking the arrows next to the Date column.

Each document includes a name and description (when provided by the court). If a document is already available in the shared library, its name will appear highlighted, allowing you to open the PDF directly in the viewer. On the right side of each document, you’ll see options to download or purchase:

  • A download icon indicates the document is already available and can be downloaded as a PDF at no additional cost.
  • A green button with a price allows you to purchase the document from the court.
  • If the price is $0.00, the document is free and will be ordered at no cost.
  • If a cost is listed, that amount will be charged to your account upon purchase. If it is a Federal court document you will need a linked PACER account to order and the cost will be passed directly to your PACER account.

Most document orders are fulfilled within 30–60 seconds, though timing may vary depending on the court system and file size.

You can also select multiple documents using the checkboxes on the left side to place bulk orders.

Parties/Attorneys Tab

The Parties & Attorneys tab lists all parties associated with the case, along with their assigned roles (such as plaintiff or defendant). For each party, you’ll also see their attorney of record and the attorney’s law firm when available in the court metadata.

If any party, attorney, or law firm appears highlighted, it has been normalized and linked within the system. Clicking on these links will take you to that entity’s profile page, where you can explore additional details and analytics.

Judges Tab

The judges tab lists all judges associated with the case in the court meta data. If a judge appears highlighted, it has been normalized and linked within the system. Clicking on these links will take you to that judge’s profile page, where you can explore additional details and analytics.

Related Cases Tab

The Related Cases tab displays any cases that the court has associated with the current case. These relationships may include cases that are part of a multidistrict litigation (MDL), class actions, formally consolidated matters, appeals, or sister cases (such as those filed across multiple court systems in jurisdictions like New York). Use this tab to quickly identify and navigate to connected cases, giving you a broader view of how the matter fits into related litigation.

Docket History Tab

The Docket History tab provides a complete audit trail of all information ingested for the case. Each entry reflects a specific update, showing when new data was pulled and what information was added, removed, or changed. UniCourt tracks all updates to the case, including docket entries, documents, and any changes to parties, attorneys, or judges. Entries are color-coded to help you quickly understand activity:

  • Green indicates new information was added
  • Red indicates information was removed
  • Blue indicates existing information was updated

For each entry, the court filing date is displayed on the left, while the timestamp of when the information was ingested is shown on the right, giving you clear visibility into both the source event and when it was captured.

Similar Cases Tab

The Similar Cases tab displays a list of other cases in the same court that share key characteristics with the current case, including the same judge and case type. This helps you quickly identify comparable litigation, providing useful context and insights into how similar matters are being handled within that court.

Entity Pages

DART provides detailed profiles and analytics for all normalized entities, including attorneys, law firms, judges, and parties. These pages offer insights into activity, case history, and performance to help you better understand each entity.

You can access an entity profile by searching for it using the appropriate search type, or by clicking on a linked name from a case page where the entity appears.

Profile header

At the top, a role badge (for example Attorney) appears beside the normalized name in large serif-style type. Below that, Law Firm and Location lines use icons for quick scanning. On the right, actions such as Unpin from Project use an outlined button with icon and blue label text.

Below the header, a series of tabs organizes detailed insights:

  • Summary: Highlights key metrics including total cases, party representation (types of clients represented), filings by year, filings by case type (standardized across courts), and filings by individual courts at both the state and federal level.
  • Profile tab: Contains biographical and professional details collected from state bar sources, including contact information, bar admissions (state, number, status, and admitted date), employment history (most recent shown), law school information, and any disciplinary records. Full employment history available through API.
  • Judgments tab: (New York only): Provides judgment and verdict analytics, including total judgments, win rates, percentage of cases reaching judgment or verdict, total and median award amounts, and breakdowns by year, timing, case type, and judgment type.
  • Attorneys tab: Shows opposing counsel, with a visualization of the top 10 and a full list below. Each attorney is clickable to view their profile.
  • Law Firms tab: Lists firms the attorney has been associated with.
  • Parties tab: Displays normalized parties the attorney has represented (only companies and corporate entities are normalized and listed). Lists judges the attorney has appeared before.
  • Judges tab: Lists judges the attorney has appeared before.
  • Cases tab: A complete list of normalized cases involving the attorney, fully searchable and filterable.

Tracking

The Case Tracking feature allows you to monitor cases automatically and stay informed of new activity without manually checking for updates. When you set a track on a case, UniCourt will refresh it at scheduled intervals based on your selected frequency. If an update is found, the case is updated in the platform, an in-product notification is generated, and an email notification is sent to all users in the associated workspace.

Set Tracking

To start tracking a case, click the Set Tracker button in the top right of any Case Page.

This opens a setup window where you can choose how often the case should be checked—ranging from monthly to high-frequency tracking. By default, high-frequency tracking runs every three hours between 8 AM and 8 PM (local court hours), but you can customize it up to once per hour. You can also define a start date (default is the current day) and an optional end date. Once configured, click Set Tracker to activate tracking.

You’ll receive a confirmation notification once it’s live.

You can manage and review tracked cases from the Tracking page, accessible via the sidebar in the shortcut menu for all tracked cases across workspaces, or in tracking under the active workspace for a workspace specific view of tracking. Inside the Tracking Page there are three tabs:

  • New Updates: Shows unread updates with a snapshot of recent docket activity. Click a case name to open it, or mark updates as read when finished.
  • Tracked Cases: Lists all active tracks, where you can open cases, adjust tracking schedules, or cancel tracking.
  • Tracking History: Provides a log of tracking activity, including when tracks ran, how many cases tracks succeeded, how many updates were found, and any failures.

    • Tracking views are consistent across all workspaces, with workspace-specific views filtered accordingly.

    To edit or cancel tracking, click the tracking button again on the Case Page (which will display the current schedule), or manage it directly from the Tracked Cases tab.

    By default, all users in a workspace receive email notifications for tracked cases. To adjust who receives notifications, add or remove users from the workspace. Individual users can also manage or disable email notifications in their User Settings.

Pins

The Pins feature allows you to save and share important cases and entity profiles for quick access within a workspace. You can pin cases, attorneys, law firms, judges, and parties to keep key items easily accessible for you and your team.

To create a pin, click the Pin to Workspace button in the top right of any case.

You can also add a pint from an entity page.

This will add a shortcut to the currently active workspace. All users with access to that workspace will be able to see and use the pin.

You can view pins in two ways:

  • All Pins: Access a complete list of pins across all workspaces from the shortcut menu.
  • Workspace Pins: View pins specific to your active workspace using the pins icon in the sidebar.

Within the Pins page, you'll see a list of all pinned items, including cases and entities. You can search for specific pins using the search bar or filter by type (case, attorney, law firm, judge, or party) using the options at the top of the page. Each pin displays the item name and its associated workspace.

To remove a pin, click the Unpin icon on the pin card or unpin directly from the case or profile page.

Pins are shared at the workspace level, so any user added to a workspace will automatically have access to its pins. To share pins with additional users, simply add them to the workspace through the Manage option in the sidebar.

Analytics

The Analytics section in UniCourt DART provides powerful insights into litigation trends and performance across the platform. You can access analytics in two ways: by searching for a specific entity (to view analytics tailored to that entity) or by navigating to the Analytics tab in the sidebar for broader, platform-wide insights.

Within Analytics, there are three main tabs:

  • Case Count Analytics: Offers a high-level view of all cases in the UniCourt database (note: analytics include data from 2000 onward). This tab displays cases by case type, filings by year, and cases by state, with the option to toggle between federal and state-level litigation. Select a filter to navigate to a specific state or case type for a more indepth look at litigation trends.
  • Judgment Analytics: Currently available for New York Supreme Courts, this tab provides a 10-year overview of judgment and verdict activity. It includes total cases, judgments, and verdicts, along with win percentages, total and median award amounts, and breakdowns by year, timing, case type, and judgment type. This can be filtered by judgment type, case type, filing date, or court, to get a tailored look at trends that matter to you.
  • Attorney Analytics: Features a comparison tool for attorneys who have served as attorney of record on cases with judgments. By default, attorneys are ranked by judgments won, with additional metrics such as case count, judgment win percentage, percentage of cases reaching judgment, total and median award amounts, and time to judgment. Similar metrics are also available for verdicts.

    • The attorney comparison tool is fully interactive—search for specific attorneys, use filters (filing date, case type, court, judgment type, and party role), and click Compare to pin attorneys to the top for side-by-side analysis. You can also expand the list to view up to 100 attorneys per page, helping you identify the most relevant attorneys for your research.
