Complaint Summary

auto_awesome

John Hummel, individually and as Administrator of the Estate of Eleanor Hummel, brings this action under Article 15 of the New York Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law (RPAPL) to cancel and discharge a mortgage encumbering the Estate's property at 5489 Federal Road, Conesus, New York. Eleanor Hummel granted a $50,000 mortgage to Homeamerican Credit, Inc. d/b/a Upland Mortgage in 2000. After her death in 2009, the mortgage passed through a chain of six assignments ending with defendant Cilici, LLC. On June 22, 2011, Omat I REO Holdings accelerated the entire balance by filing a foreclosure action, and no effective de-acceleration occurred within the following six years. Plaintiff alleges the six-year statute of limitations to foreclose therefore expired on or about June 21, 2017, and further that Cilici never legally acquired the note and mortgage. Plaintiff seeks cancellation and discharge of the time-barred mortgage under RPAPL § 1501(4) and a judgment barring Cilici from any claim to the property.

Legal Citations

RPAPL Article 15 RPAPL § 1501(4) Real Property Law § 319
