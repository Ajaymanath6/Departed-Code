Case Summary

Fewel v. Kia Motors America, Inc. is a consumer protection case filed in Alameda County Superior Court (Department 517), assigned to Judge Somnath Raj Chatterjee. Plaintiff Michael Fewel brings claims against Kia Motors America under the Song-Beverly Consumer Warranty Act, alleging that Kia failed to repair or repurchase a defective vehicle within a reasonable number of attempts. Four motions have been filed, three of which have been decided. Alternative Motion for Summary Adjudication was granted in part on the Second Cause of Action only.

Summary of Complaint

Plaintiff Michael Fewel filed suit against Kia Motors America, Inc. asserting six causes of action under the Song-Beverly Consumer Warranty Act arising from the purchase of a defective vehicle that Kia allegedly failed to repair to conform to applicable warranties. Order re: Motion for Summary Judgment — 2026-03-19

The Court, having taken this matter under submission following oral argument on March 14, 2025, hereby issues its Decision and Order. Defendant Reynolds' Motion for Summary Judgment pursuant to CPLR § 3212 is DENIED in its entirety. Defendant has not met the initial burden of tendering sufficient evidence to demonstrate the absence of a material issue of fact. The contract ambiguity regarding delivery specifications and the disputed invoice communications create triable issues on the First and Second Causes of Action.

The Court further finds that triable issues of material fact exist as to the breach of contract and unjust enrichment claims. The motion is denied without prejudice to renewal following completion of discovery.
