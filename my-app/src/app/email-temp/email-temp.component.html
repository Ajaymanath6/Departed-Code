New Announcement

Aug 6, 2026

Dear John,

Introducing an exciting new upgrade: DEEP v3

A major upgrade to the DEEP API platform is now ready for your account!

New API capabilities

- Case Track — scheduled case updates, up to hourly
- Case Import — bring cases in from court sources
- Document Text Search — full-text search across hundreds of millions of documents
- Case Match & Normalized Entity Match — fuzzy-match your internal records
- Case History — a full change log across parties, counsel, and dockets
- Court Service Status Notifications — know when court services go down or come back

Account usage management

View and manage your account's usage across DEEP and DART in one place. Workspaces are now shared between the two, so activity and usage stay consistent no matter which platform your team is working from.

- New usage API — view usage by account or by workspace
- Flexible token management — including workspace-scoped tokens to control access the way your team is organized

New entity normalization system

Extra-refined attorney & law firm profiles, with fewer duplicates/fragments and even clearer connections between attorneys, firms, and the cases they're on.

Full Release Notes

What this means for you

Your DART access will not change, as this upgrade is specific to your API workflow and will enable you to move to DEEP v3.

- What's involved — pointing your integration to the new endpoints, including workspace support. Some schema changes may apply depending on which objects you use. Your previous API version will keep running in parallel, so your team can migrate whenever it makes sense for you, though upgrading earlier will allow you to take advantage of the new features and benefits as soon as possible.
- Support along the way — our team is ready to help you plan and test your transition, whether that's your app, your API integration, or both.
